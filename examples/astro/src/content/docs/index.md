description: A small Astro site that diglot translates into German and Japanese.
---

This site is written in English and translated into German and Japanese by [diglot](https://github.com/danielroe/diglot). Every translation is a record in the `translations` directory, and the loader turns those records into pages when Astro builds the site.

Read the [guide](guide/) to see how a page is translated.
