---
title: Welcome
description: A small Angular app that diglot translates into French and Turkish.
---

This app is written in English and translated into French and Turkish by [diglot](https://github.com/danielroe/diglot). Every translation is a record in the `translations` directory, and `@diglot/angular` applies those records when a page loads.

Read the [guide](guide) to see how a page is translated.
